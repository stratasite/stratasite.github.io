Strata can delegate sign-in to any SAML 2.0 identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Ping, Keycloak, and others). This page covers the values Strata needs and what to register back at your IdP.

For shared behavior — how users are auto-created, license seats, and the break-glass password fallback — see [Authentication & SSO](/docs/self-hosting/authentication/).

## How it works

1. A user clicks **Sign in with SSO** on the Strata login page.
2. Strata sends an **unsigned** SAML AuthnRequest to your IdP.
3. The user authenticates at the IdP.
4. The IdP POSTs a **signed** SAML assertion back to Strata's Assertion Consumer Service (ACS) URL.
5. Strata verifies the assertion's signature against the IdP certificate you configured, then signs the user in (creating the account on first login).

Strata verifies the IdP's response signature — that is what secures the flow. Strata does **not** sign its own request, so your IdP must not require a signed request from the service provider (see Troubleshooting).

## What to register at your IdP

Create a SAML application (service provider) at your IdP with:

- **Entity ID / Audience** that matches Strata's **SP entity ID** (default `strata`).
- **ACS / Reply URL**:

  ```text
  https://YOUR_STRATA_HOST/auth/saml/callback
  ```

- **Sign assertions**: enabled (Strata verifies the signature).
- **Require signed requests / client signature**: disabled.
- **Encrypt assertions**: disabled (Strata expects signed, not encrypted, assertions).

## Attribute mapping

The assertion must release the user's **email**. Strata reads the standard `info` attributes and recognizes these attribute names:

| Identity field | Accepted SAML attribute names |
|---|---|
| Email (required) | `email`, `mail` |
| First name | `first_name`, `firstname`, `firstName` |
| Last name | `last_name`, `lastname`, `lastName` |

If first/last name are missing, Strata derives the name from the email local part. If email is missing, the login fails — make sure your IdP releases an email attribute.

## Fields in Strata

In **System Settings → SSO**, choose method **SAML 2.0** and fill in:

| Field | Required | What it is / where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| **IdP SSO service URL** | Yes | Your IdP's SAML 2.0 single sign-on endpoint (sometimes called "SSO URL" or "SAML 2.0 endpoint"). Found in your IdP's SAML app settings or IdP metadata. |
| **IdP certificate (PEM)** | Yes | Your IdP's **signing** certificate, PEM-wrapped (see below). |
| **SP entity ID** | No (default `strata`) | Must equal the Entity ID / Audience you set on the IdP application. Leave blank to use `strata`. |
| **Assertion consumer service URL** | No | Defaults to `https://YOUR_STRATA_HOST/auth/saml/callback`. Set only to override. |

The **IdP certificate** must include the PEM header and footer:

```text
-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----
MIIC...your IdP signing certificate...
-----END CERTIFICATE-----
```

Most IdPs let you download or copy this certificate from the SAML app's settings or from the IdP's SAML metadata document. Use the **signing** certificate, not an encryption certificate.

Click **Save SSO settings**, then restart all Strata application servers.

## Troubleshooting

### The IdP shows "invalid signature" or "SigAlg was null"

Your IdP is configured to **require a signed request** from the service provider. Strata sends unsigned AuthnRequests (standard for this integration — the signed *assertion* is what secures the flow). Disable "require signed requests" / "client signature required" on the IdP application.

### The IdP shows "invalid requester" or an entity-ID error

Strata's **SP entity ID** (default `strata`) must exactly match the Entity ID / Audience configured on the IdP application. Make them identical.

### The IdP shows "invalid redirect / ACS URL"

Register the exact ACS URL on the IdP application:

```text
https://YOUR_STRATA_HOST/auth/saml/callback
```

Confirm `APP_HOST` and `APP_PROTOCOL` match the public URL users browse to.

### The user reaches Strata but login fails on a blank email

The assertion isn't releasing an email attribute. Add an attribute mapper at the IdP that emits the user's email (attribute name `email` or `mail`), and confirm the IdP user actually has an email set.

### Login fails after an encryption change

Strata expects **signed** assertions, not **encrypted** ones. Disable assertion encryption on the IdP application and keep assertion signing enabled.
